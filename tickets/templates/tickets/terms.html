Any activity performed under a user account is considered the responsibility of the account owner.

Acceptable Use

Users agree to use the system responsibly and professionally.

Users must NOT:

  • Submit false or misleading tickets

  • Interfere with system operations or security

  • Share confidential information without authorization

Violation of these rules may result in account suspension or disciplinary action.

Ticket Management

Ticket Submission

Users must provide clear and accurate details when creating tickets to ensure proper assistance and resolution.

Ticket Assignment

The system may automatically or manually assign tickets to ICT personnel based on availability, workload, or responsibility.

Ticket Status

Users can monitor ticket progress through statuses such as:

  • Pending
  • Ongoing
  • Resolved
  • Closed

Administrators may update or modify ticket records when necessary for operational purposes.

Roles and Permissions

  • ICT Head: Full administrative access. Manage users, tickets, and reports. Monitor employee performance and activity.
  • ICT Secretary: Assist in ticket monitoring and documentation. Manage communications and records.
  • Employee: Handle assigned tickets and provide technical assistance. Update ticket progress and resolutions.
  • Complainant/User: Submit concerns and monitor ticket status.

Users may not attempt to access functions outside their assigned permissions.
