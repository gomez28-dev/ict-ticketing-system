Privacy Policy

ICT Helpdesk Management System

Last Updated: May 2026

Introduction

Your privacy is important to us. The ICT Helpdesk Management System is committed to protecting the personal information of all users including employees, school personnel, ICT staff, and administrators. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when using the system. By accessing and using the ICT Helpdesk Management System, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide efficient technical support and system management services.

Personal Information

The system may collect the following information during account registration and usage:

  • Full Name
  • School or Office Assignment
  • Official DepEd Email Address
  • Contact Number
  • User Role or Position
  • Login Credentials

Ticket and Service Information

When submitting support requests, the system may collect:

  • Ticket Details and Descriptions
  • Uploaded Files or Attachments
  • Assigned Personnel Information
  • Ticket Status and Resolution History
  • Time and Date Records

System Usage Data

The system may automatically record:

  • Login Activity
  • Device and Browser Information
  • IP Address
  • Access Time and Usage Logs

How We Use Information

The collected information is used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Process ICT support requests and service tickets
  • Assign tickets to ICT personnel
  • Monitor employee availability and workload
  • Generate reports and analytics
  • Improve system performance and user experience
  • Maintain system security and prevent unauthorized access
  • Communicate system updates, notices, and resolutions

Account and Access Management

Only authorized users are allowed to access the system.

  • Users must register using an official DepEd email account.
  • Account requests are reviewed and approved by authorized administrators.
  • Different access permissions are assigned based on user roles such as ICT Head, ICT Secretary, Employee, and Complainant/User.

Unauthorized access, misuse of accounts, or sharing of credentials is strictly prohibited.

Data Protection and Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, data loss, alteration or disclosure, and misuse of system records.

Security measures may include:

  • Encrypted login credentials
  • Role-based access control
  • Secure database storage
  • Activity and audit logs
  • Session monitoring

Data Retention

Information and ticket records are retained only for operational, reporting, and auditing purposes. Data may be archived or deleted when no longer necessary, subject to applicable policies and regulations.

Sharing of Information

The ICT Helpdesk Management System does not sell or share personal information with unauthorized third parties. Information may only be accessed by authorized personnel responsible for technical support, administration, and system maintenance.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or official government regulations.

User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate information
  • Maintaining the confidentiality of their accounts
  • Using the system responsibly and ethically
  • Reporting suspicious activities or unauthorized access immediately

Cookies and System Monitoring

The system may use session cookies and monitoring tools to maintain secure logins, improve functionality, and analyze system performance. These tools do not collect unnecessary personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements, security updates, or changes in regulations. Users will be notified of significant updates within the system.

Contact Information

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, users may contact the ICT Helpdesk System Administration through the official ICT office communication channels.

Consent

By using the ICT Helpdesk Management System, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy.
